Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Private Room?

Tap "Private Room" on the home screen, select your game settings, and tap "Create". Share the 6-digit code with your friends so they can join.

How do I join a Private Room?

Tap "Private Room" on the home screen, enter the 6-digit code shared by the host, and tap "Join".

What is a Wild Card?

At the start of each round a random card is selected as the Wild Card. Cards of that rank from any suit act as wild cards and can substitute for any card in a sequence or set.

How do I declare?

Arrange your 13 cards into valid groups — at least one pure sequence (no wild cards) is required. Tap "Declare" and arrange your cards into groups to confirm your winning hand.

What is a pure sequence?

A pure sequence is 3 or more consecutive cards of the same suit with no wild cards. Every valid declaration must contain at least one pure sequence.

How do I delete my account?

To request account deletion and removal of all associated data, please email us at support@deckmatesrummy.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
